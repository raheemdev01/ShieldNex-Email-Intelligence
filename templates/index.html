03 / INTERPRETATION

Understand the signal.

Each result includes the evidence behind the classification—without claiming mailbox-level certainty.

✓

Delivered*

Address syntax is valid and domain mail-routing evidence was found. The mailbox and final delivery are not confirmed.

×

Likely to Bounce

The address format is invalid or a strong domain failure signal was returned. The explanation states the reason.

?

Unknown

DNS restrictions, timeouts, or ambiguous responses prevent a reliable conclusion.

i

Methodology note
This tool does not send email or probe mailboxes. DNS checks can be blocked by hosting providers; when the evidence is inconclusive, the app returns Unknown. Repeated addresses remain in the output, while repeated domain lookups are cached within a batch.
